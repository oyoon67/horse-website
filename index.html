All about horses

What are horses like?

Horses are large mammals that have lived alongside humans for thousands of years. They are herd animals, which means they naturally enjoy company. They communicate using sounds, body language, facial expressions, and movement.

🧠

Intelligent

Horses can learn routines, recognize familiar people, remember experiences, and respond to training based on clear and kind communication.

❤️

Social

They often feel comfortable around other horses and can build bonds with people. Friendship and trust are important parts of their lives.

👀

Very Aware

Horses are naturally alert. Their senses help them notice changes around them, so calm surroundings and gentle handling matter.

Horse personality

Every horse is different.

Just like people, individual horses can have different personalities. One may be calm and quiet while another is playful and full of energy.

🌿
GentleMany horses can be calm and affectionate when they feel safe and comfortable.
✨
CuriousHorses often investigate new sights, sounds, and objects in their environment.
⚡
EnergeticThey naturally enjoy moving, running, playing, and exploring open spaces.
🤝
TrustingWith patient, respectful handling, horses can learn to feel secure around people.

🐴 How do horses communicate?

They use their ears, eyes, head position, tail, posture, sounds, and movement. For example, their ears can help show where their attention is focused. Learning to notice these signals is an important part of understanding horses.

Taking care of horses

What do horses need?

Good horse care means meeting their physical, social, and emotional needs every day.

🥕 Food & water
Horses need appropriate nutrition and constant access to clean water.
🌳 Space & movement
Safe opportunities to move and explore are important for their wellbeing.
🧹 Grooming
Regular grooming helps keep a horse clean and gives handlers a chance to notice changes.
🩺 Health care
Veterinary, dental, hoof, and other routine care help keep horses healthy.
